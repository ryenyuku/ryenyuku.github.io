"Ahh.. The beauty of life," the morning sunrise met your eyes as you said the sentence. You get up, walked into the bathroom and enjoyed the fresh water running down your body in the shower. You then proceeded to brush your teeth and you saw the white and glowing teeth of yours while you're grinning to yourself. You dried yourself and wore a uniform, preparing to go to school/work. As you went to the kitchen you met your parents/wife/family with honest smile in their faces as they prepare the food. You helped them cook and you drooled just by looking at the food. You went to the dining room and ate the food together while talking about random & interesting topics that came to your mind. You said goodbye to them, and you kissed your parents/wife and kids.

As you went off the house, you said hi to your neighbours and they politely replied back, sometimes even making small conversations with them. You went to the school/work and on the way you see just how busy the street can be. You met your friends/coworkers, and you both laughed while talking about the embarrassing moments you both saw yesterday.

After a long day, you went home and thought of a place to go for a vacation that you deserved. Maybe you thought of a beach to enjoy the warm and refreshing water, a museum to look at and appreciate history, a laboratory to wonder about the micro worlds, space observatory to be in awe with how large and beautiful the universe is, a theatre/art exhibition to value creativity and culture, or a cinema to watch something that you like. Maybe you want to be adventurous and go hiking to see the beautiful mountains, or be simple and just observe the cosmos from afar on the top of your roof. There are a lot of beautiful things in this life, and it is impossible for such a complex design to exist without a creator. But for some, this world is cruel to them, even to a degree where they want to kill themselves. And the question is, why?

Have you ever thought of dying, maybe to an extent where you have planned and executed it but failed at the end? You're not alone in this regard, but why do people feel this way despite the beautiful things in this world? Turns out this world is not all about happy things. After all "happiness" can only coexist with "sadness", right? But why does it feel like this sadness won't come away, why does happiness seem so hard to reach?

Suicidal people, and those who are hurt, all developed some kind of depression. The cause can vary, be it psychological like a trauma that never healed, loss of someone they loved, loneliness, harassment, stress & pressure, anxiety, low self-esteem, chronic illness, or even it can be physiological like genetic, hormonal changes and neurotransmitter imbalance. These conditions will manifest in physical deterioration, known as psychosomatic disorder, that if left untreated can be fatal. These psychosomatic disorder in turn can contribute back to depression. Depression, anxiety and other related psychological and/or physiological can be treatable or even curable, but what if one does not have an easy access to treatment?

Victims of genocide/war, broken home, child abuse, economically unstable or even just scared to seek professional help for various reasons, can't find an easy way to get themselves treated. So is there any other options? Unlike depression caused by physiological disorder, one that's caused by psychological truly depends on yourself. What you believe will affect how you act, understand, cope, and resolve with a situation. Sometimes we just can't change what's in stone. So we adapt, we change, and we must believe on ourselves. When you truly believe on yourself, you'll not give up easily, you'll believe that you're capable and you'll learn through all the obstacles until you've reached your goals. But believing on yourself is not easy especially when everything seems to be bleak, is it?

The universe and everything is already perfect, but not the humans. Greed, corruption, hatred and evil are all the result of mankind. But does that mean that there is no hope anymore? Obviously not, after all the meaning of honesty, kindness and good also exists because of them. There are still a lot of faithful people, people who are aware of the injustice, and the victims of it. We need to strive not because we can or can't, but because we have to. We must strive because we have to help the weak. We must believe we can see a better future and we can make it happen. History has taught us that no matter how bleak the situation is, we can persevere, because we are promised that with every hardship comes ease. We must endure the process and failures, because we will get what we wanted. We must hope, and by dying we can't do anything for it to happen. We will make it.

So live. Live if it hurts, live if it seems hopeless, live if everything seems dark. Because we must see through everything, because we must see our victory, because we can change, and because we're strong. And if you did survive to the end, don't forget to thank yourself. If it's possible, hug yourself and cry all you want, because you deserved it.

And lastly.. Thank YOU for reading to the end.
